The Living Christians Sanctuary

A sanctuary of the FGO family under King Fodan's oversight, connected through this same portal for member records and ministry training.

What this prototype does

Members can register and submit prayer or welfare requests. Intern pastors can enroll in Bible School courses and sit exams. Admin can review members, welfare requests, and exam results. Sermons and Sunday School material are published for members to read.
